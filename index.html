Meet the Voice

About Taylor

Taylor Norton is a Vancouver-based voice actor who brings the warmth, candor, and easygoing energy of a trusted friend into every session. Whether he’s breathing life into an eccentric animated character, grounding a high-stakes video game world, or guiding listeners through an engaging topic, Taylor’s reads are defined by an approachable authority and unmistakable authenticity.

A storyteller at heart since his first middle school stage production, Taylor spent years honing his craft under the theatre lights before earning his Diploma in Theatre from the University of the Fraser Valley. He later brought that deep theatrical foundation directly to the booth, graduating from the intensive Full-Time Voice Over Diploma program at On The Mic Training.

As a lifelong enthusiast of animation, video games, and dubbing, Taylor pairs genuine fan knowledge with disciplined vocal technique—knowing when to play big and dynamic, and when to settle into an intimate, conversational read for narration and commercials.
